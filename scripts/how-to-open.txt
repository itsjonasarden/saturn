Akyba
======

1. Drag Akyba into Applications.

2. Open it once from Applications. macOS says it can't check Akyba for malicious
   software, because Akyba isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID. Click Done.

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to the line about Akyba. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

From then on Akyba opens like any other app.

Akyba runs Claude Code in its terminals, so install that first:
https://claude.com/claude-code
